# ASPN Platform Build Manual Addendum v0.5 Simplified

## Purpose

This simplified addendum explains what was added to the ASPN Platform in v0.5 in plain language for ASPN leadership, educators, community stakeholders, and partner organizations.

## Current Status

The ASPN Platform is now **pilot-capable**.

This means the platform has the major tools needed to prepare for and manage a controlled youth pilot with staff oversight. It does not mean the platform is ready for broad public launch.

## What Was Added

### Educator Directory

ASPN staff can now keep track of educators and school contacts.

This helps ASPN organize relationships with:
- schools
- counselors
- teachers
- college contacts
- nonprofit educators
- education partners

### Partner Organization Directory

ASPN staff can now track nonprofit, community, workforce, business, foundation, higher education, faith-based, and other partner organizations.

This helps staff understand which organizations are involved in the youth development ecosystem.

### Government Organization Directory

ASPN staff can now track public-sector and government organizations.

This supports long-term civic learning, public service, workforce, and Future Ready planning.

### Relationship Notes

ASPN staff can now record relationship notes across educators, partner organizations, and government organizations.

These notes can track:
- conversations
- partnership stage
- follow-up dates
- staff owner
- relationship history

This is not a messaging system. It is an internal staff recordkeeping tool.

### Stakeholder Reporting

Staff can now see summaries of relationship activity, including:
- upcoming follow-ups
- overdue follow-ups
- notes by stakeholder type
- notes by relationship status
- relationship ownership by staff UID

### Pilot Readiness Dashboard

The readiness dashboard helps answer:

**Are we ready?**

It shows:
- readiness score
- readiness status
- blockers
- warnings
- checklist items

### Pilot Metrics Dashboard

The metrics dashboard helps answer:

**What is happening?**

It shows:
- registrations
- active users
- program participation
- credentials
- attendance
- service hours
- stakeholder activity
- staff activity

### Pilot Evaluation Dashboard

The evaluation dashboard helps answer:

**How well is the pilot performing?**

It shows:
- youth outcomes
- program outcomes
- credential outcomes
- service outcomes
- stakeholder outcomes
- operations outcomes
- strengths
- concerns
- recommended actions

## What the Platform Can Now Support

The platform can now support:
- youth account creation
- youth profile completion
- program enrollment
- credential viewing
- staff credential awarding
- attendance tracking
- service-hour tracking
- RWD learning tracking
- notifications
- staff dashboards
- educator management
- partner management
- government organization management
- stakeholder relationship notes
- pilot readiness review
- pilot metrics review
- pilot evaluation review

## What the Platform Does Not Do Yet

The platform does not yet include:
- a mobile app
- public youth profiles
- youth messaging
- social networking
- public search
- educator portals
- partner portals
- government portals
- CRM integration
- exports
- workforce matching
- scholarship matching
- full UX/UI redesign

## Pilot Use

The platform is intended for a controlled pilot with staff oversight. Previous planning references a pilot range of 60–100 youth participants, with early onboarding likely starting smaller so staff can monitor operations carefully.
